# Request Prioritisation

## Priority levels
- **Critical**: risk to life within hours, such as medical emergencies, people stranded on
  rooftops, or no drinking water for a camp.
- **High**: needs that must be met the same day, such as drinking water or food for displaced
  families.
- **Medium**: needs within 24 hours, such as shelter kits and blankets.
- **Low**: replenishment and non-urgent items.

## Competing requests
When two requests compete for the same stock or team, serve the higher priority first. Within the
same priority, serve the larger number of vulnerable people first.
