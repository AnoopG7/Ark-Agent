# Road Status and Closures

## Status meanings
- **Open**: normal travel time applies.
- **Slow**: passable with delays; add a 50 percent buffer to the listed travel time when judging
  deadlines.
- **Blocked**: impassable. Do not plan any movement over a blocked route, even if it is the
  shortest.

## Reporting
When a coordinator or team reports a change in road conditions, record it as a road report in
operational memory so the next shift sees it, and ask the control room to update the route
status.

## Re-routing
If a plan's route becomes blocked before departure, the plan must be re-made and re-approved.
