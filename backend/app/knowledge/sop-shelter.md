# Emergency Shelter and Non-Food Items

## Shelter kits
One shelter kit (tarpaulin, rope, ground sheet) serves **one household of five people**.
Divide the number of people by five and round up.

## Blankets
Provide **one blanket per person**, two per person for children under five and the elderly when
night temperatures fall below 18 °C.

## Teams
Shelter kits are installed by a team with the **shelter** skill; logistics teams may deliver but
not install them.
