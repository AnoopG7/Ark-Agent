# Shift Handover and Operational Memory

## What to record
Record every approved dispatch, every rejection with its reason, every stock reservation and every
road report in operational memory. Write facts in one sentence with the ward, quantity and depot,
for example "Depot B reserved 200 litres of water for Ward 7".

## Starting a shift
Before planning, review remembered facts for the ward in question to avoid duplicate dispatches
and repeated checks.

## Duplicate requests
If a request repeats one already fulfilled in the last 12 hours, tell the coordinator what was
already sent and ask whether an additional dispatch is needed.
