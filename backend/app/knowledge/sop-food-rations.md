# Food Ration Distribution

## Ration size
One food ration packet feeds **one person for one day** (about 2,100 kcal: rice, lentils, oil,
salt and biscuits). Plan rations for the number of people multiplied by the number of days.

## Special diets
Infants under one year must not receive standard rations; request infant formula from the
district medical store. Diabetic and elderly beneficiaries receive the soft-diet variant where
available.

## Distribution
Food is distributed together with drinking water whenever both are requested for the same ward,
using one team and one trip where vehicle capacity allows. A logistics team is required for any
distribution above 200 packets.
