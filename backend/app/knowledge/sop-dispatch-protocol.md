# Dispatch Planning and Approval Protocol

## Planning steps
1. Confirm the ward, resource, number of people and deadline.
2. Calculate the quantity needed using the relevant supply SOP.
3. Check stock at every depot, then check routes from those depots to the ward.
4. Choose an available team whose vehicle matches the route and whose skills match the cargo.
5. Propose the dispatch for coordinator approval.

## Route selection
Prefer the fastest usable route. Never plan over a blocked route. When the fastest depot cannot
reach the ward, use the next depot with a usable route and accept the longer travel time.

## Estimating arrival
ETA is the longest travel time among the routes used plus 15 minutes for loading. "By evening"
means within six hours of the request; flag plans whose ETA exceeds the deadline.

## Approval
No team may be dispatched and no stock reserved without explicit coordinator approval. A rejected
plan is recorded with the coordinator's reason and must be revised before it is proposed again.

## Insufficient stock
If total available stock is below the requirement, propose the best partial plan, state the
shortfall in units and people not covered, and recommend escalation to the district control room.
