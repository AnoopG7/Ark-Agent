# Boat Operations on Flooded Routes

## When boats are required
Routes marked with vehicle type **boat** can only be travelled by boat. A team assigned to such a
route must have the **boat_rescue** skill and a boat vehicle. Trucks must never be assigned to a
boat-only route.

## Capacity
Each relief boat carries at most **500 litres of water or 150 ration packets** per trip. Plan
multiple trips or multiple boats for larger loads and add 30 minutes per extra trip to the ETA.

## Safety limits
Suspend boat operations when wind exceeds 40 km/h or after sunset unless a rescue is under way.
Every crew member wears a life jacket; a boat carries no more than six people including crew.
