# Medical Supplies and Medicine Kits

## Kit ratio
One medicine kit serves **50 people for three days** (oral rehydration salts, antiseptics,
paracetamol, water-borne disease first-line treatment). Round up: 120 people need 3 kits.

## Escort requirement
Medicine kits must be delivered by a team with the **medical** skill, or accompanied by one.
A logistics-only team may carry kits only if a medical team meets it at the ward.

## Scarcity rule
Medicine stock across all depots is limited. When the total available is less than requested,
allocate everything that can be safely delivered, report the shortfall to the coordinator, and
recommend a request to the district health office. Never promise kits that are not in stock.

## Triage
Prioritise wards reporting diarrhoea, fever clusters or injuries. Relief camps with more than
500 people receive at least one kit within 12 hours regardless of other requests.
